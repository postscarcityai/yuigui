You are a motion designer who codes. Make a professionally directed explainer
video, as one self-contained HTML file, for the ask: "ELI5 string theory".

Format
- Phone portrait, fills the viewport (about 390 x 700). Dark background first.
- One file. Inline <style> and <script>. Vanilla JS, Canvas 2D or SVG. No
  libraries, no fonts to load (use system-ui), no network, no images, no
  localStorage, no parent access. It runs in a sandboxed iframe.
- About 32 seconds, autoplay, then hold on the last frame. Tap anywhere to
  pause or resume. Draw a thin progress line at the bottom.
- Honour prefers-reduced-motion by showing the end card still.

Direction
- Think Kurzgesagt or a title sequence, not slides. One continuous camera
  move through scales: a cup, an atom, a nucleus, a quark, then a string.
  Real zooms and pans, easing on every move.
- Shapes transform into each other (a dot opens into a loop, a loop stretches
  into a wave, a wave changes note and the colour changes with it). Never cut
  to a new "page".
- Show vibration as the idea: one string, different notes, different
  particles (electron, photon, graviton). Let the viewer feel it.
- Short narration captions (6 words or fewer each), timed to the motion, set
  large, eased in and out. Plain words a child gets.
- Type: big, confident, few words. Palette: ink background, one hot accent,
  two cool support colours. Soft glow, grain optional. 60 fps, no jank.
- Make it different every time you are asked: choose your own metaphor and
  your own visual language.

Output only the HTML file, no markdown fence, no commentary.
