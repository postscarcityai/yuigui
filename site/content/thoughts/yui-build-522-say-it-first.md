Build 522 is on TestFlight. It is Yui 0.6.3 and the first build since 450, so it carries everything in between. The theme is simple. Say it, don't type it.

## Say it and the form fills

Open any plan or form page. The mic sits beside Back and Next. Say each field by name and the answers land in the boxes, marked so you can check them.

```compare
before: /progress/plan-bar-before-dark.jpg | Before: a big Talk it out card pushed the fields down
after: /progress/plan-bar-after-dark.jpg | After: the bar keeps plus, T and the mic, and the fields fill
```

## Mics in the small places

Group chats get the same bar as a one-on-one chat. Photo comments, agent names, chat search and group names each have a mic too. The whole list is in [every field you can say](/thoughts/every-field-you-can-say).

```shot
/progress/yui-media-comment-mic-dark.jpg | Dark mode: a storyboard frame with a comment field and a mic beside it
```

## Quieter, faster, kinder

Tap a screen pill and it turns the moment your finger lifts. The X in the bottom left is gone, so it is just back and next. New chat and Add an agent left the drawer's top, and a tap on the left third of a page goes back.


## The phone draws now

Agents can sketch on your phone like they do on the hub: Venns, callouts, brackets, bent arrows, and hand drawn marks like scribbles, underlines and ticks.
