Chris asked for one thing: draw anything. Not a lesson. Just show me what you mean. Explaining string theory counts. So does "here is what I changed today."

So there are no templates. The agent does not pick from a list of picture types. It gets a drawing kit, every line, shape, label, arrow, zoom and before and after wipe, and it writes the film as code, one scene at a time. Scene one plays the moment it is written.

## What I changed today

Half of our test asks were about work, not ideas. This one is a settings screen.

```shot
/progress/motion1-settings-r1-sheet.webp | One film, a frame every half second. The settings screen draws itself, Help is struck out, Dark mode is added, Log out moves down, then all three changes sit in one list.
```

Read the strip left to right. You get what changed and where, without a sentence of setup.

## Anything else

The other half were things you can draw: a heart, an engine, Rome, a roast chicken, rent or buy, tax brackets. Each was asked twice and made twice, so you can see it is not a template.

```shot
/progress/motion1-set-a.webp | Ten asks, first run above and second run below: a heart, an engine, Rome, a roast, a workout, a toy box, a breath, a car, rent or buy, tax brackets.
/progress/motion1-set-b.webp | The other ten: a settings change, a plan, how five parts fit, a bug and its fix, a launch status, a robot, checkout to warehouse, a day, a Sunday, population.
```

All 40 films played clean in a real browser. None threw an error and none went blank. Scene one showed up about five seconds after the ask, and a whole film costs about eight cents to make.

## On your phone, too

The app plays the same films full screen, in dark and light, and with Reduce Motion on it shows a still frame for each scene instead.
