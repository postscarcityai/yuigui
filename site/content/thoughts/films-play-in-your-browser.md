The films you saw on the phone play on the web too. Same drawing kit, same player. Scene one starts the moment it is written, and the rest join while it plays.

We timed it for real. A real agent, a real browser, the live site. Three asks, three runs each: how a car engine works, what changed on the settings screen, how compound interest grows. The clock runs from the ask sent to the first frame.

```shot
/progress/yui312-engine-dark-film.webp | How a car engine works, playing full screen on the web, dark.
/progress/yui312-interest-dark-film.webp | How compound interest grows, dark.
```

Six of nine first frames came in under 10 seconds. The middle run was 6.7 seconds. Every film had 5 to 6 scenes and ended 32 to 60 seconds after the ask. Close put each one back in the thread as one line and a Watch again chip. No raw text showed on any run.

The three slow ones took 12.9, 20.1 and 28.7 seconds. That is the agent's film writer on a busy machine, not the page: the player starts the instant scene one lands. We wanted eight of nine, so a faster first scene is next.
