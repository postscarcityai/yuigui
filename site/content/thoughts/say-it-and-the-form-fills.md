You said you should be able to just speak your answers and have the form fill itself. Now every form card has a mic. It reaches phones with the next TestFlight build. The newest one on TestFlight today is 451, and this fix is past it. Plan pages get the same mic next.

## Before: empty fields

A form step starts the way it always did. Business name, what you do, who it is for. Nothing typed yet, and a mic waits at the top.

```shot
/progress/site182-light-1-before.webp | Light mode: the same step, three empty fields and the mic at the top.
```

## Listening: just say it

Tap the mic and talk. Say each field's name, then its answer. "Business name is Acme Bakery. What you do, we bake sourdough and pastries." Your words show on screen as you go.

```shot
/progress/site182-light-2-listening.webp | Light mode: the mic is listening and the spoken words show above the fields.
```

## Filled: check, then Next

Each answer lands in its field and gets a small mic mark, so you know which ones you spoke. Fix anything by tapping it, then go on. The speech is turned to text on your phone.

```shot
/progress/site182-light-3-filled.webp | Light mode: the three fields filled, each with a mic mark, and Next ready.
```
