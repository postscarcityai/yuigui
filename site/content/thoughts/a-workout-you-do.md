You ask for a workout. The old answer was a page to read: a list of moves and a pile of questions. Start sat on it and did very little. You do not read a workout. You do it.

## Start opens the runner

Tap Start and the whole screen is the workout. One move at a time. Nothing else on the page.

```shot
/progress/site198-plan-today-dark.webp | Dark mode: a Today's workout card with a Start button above a four move list
/progress/site198-plan-today-light.webp | Light mode: the same card, Start and the four moves
```

## Reps and weight, every set

After each set, the runner asks what you did. Chips sit around the target. A stepper nudges it. You can say it out loud.

```shot
/progress/site198-runner-log-dark.webp | Dark mode: Goblet squat, set 1 of 2 done, reps 10 and weight 20 lb with chips, a mic and a Log set button
/progress/site198-runner-log-light.webp | Light mode: the same screen, reps 10 and weight 20 lb ready to log
```

Then a rest timer runs. Add 15 seconds or skip it. Then the next set. At the end the runner sends your agent one result.

## Never in the drawer

The drawer is for finding things. A workout never plays inside it. Tap one in Review and the drawer closes, then the runner opens full screen.
