
Yui does not sell you a model. It gives you 100 turns a month to try it, and when they run out it shows one card. Add my key.

Why not just sell the model? Because the model is not the product. The screen is. A model you rent from us would be one more thing to trust, one more bill, and one more reason you could not leave. Your key is yours. So is the bill.

## One sheet, every stop

```shot
/progress/yui139-5-sheet-light.webp | The Bring your own key sheet on OpenRouter with a Save key button.
/progress/yui139-3-claude-light.webp | The key sheet on Claude, saying a Pro or Max plan cannot pay for another app, with a link to make a key.
```

Pick Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok. Or sign in with OpenRouter in one tap, with nothing to paste. Or run the agent on your own computer.

The same sheet opens from the limit card, from Settings, from Add agent and from Controls, Model. It tells you the plain thing up front: a Claude Pro or Max plan cannot pay for another app. Only an API key can.

Keys go in through Settings, never in chat. Yui never asks for one in a message or a form. The key goes to our server once, is kept locked away, and the app only shows the last four.

## A different key for each agent

```shot
/progress/yui139g-1-model-light.webp | Controls, Model, with a Runs on card offering Yui's key, Claude and ChatGPT.
/progress/yui139g-3-on-claude-light.webp | Runs on with Claude selected and its key ending in 9999.
```

Each agent gets its own pick. Put your trainer on Claude and your music agent on ChatGPT. Tap Yui's key to go back, and the saved key stays. Take a key away and its agents fall back to Yui's.

This part ships in the next build. The plan behind the free turns is in [A Yui of your own](/thoughts/a-yui-of-your-own).
