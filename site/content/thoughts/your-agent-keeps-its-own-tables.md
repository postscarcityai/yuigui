
Most agents forget. You ask for a workout, you get a paragraph, and next week it is gone. This one writes the rows down. Moves, swaps, macros, even a small CRM. Each agent keeps its own tables.

## Still there after a restart

The rows live with the agent, not in the chat. Tick a move, kill the app, open it again. The tick is still there.

```shot
/progress/yui89-workouts-relaunched.webp | Phone: the same workout after the app was killed and relaunched, rows still there.
/progress/yui89-macros-light.webp | Light, phone: macros by day, drawn from the agent's table.
```

## Same rows on the web

Open Yui in a browser tab and the coach's table is there too. Tick Split squat, reload the page, and it stays ticked. Light or dark, phone size or desktop.

```compare
before: /progress/yui296-before-reload-dark.webp | Before the reload
after: /progress/yui296-after-reload-dark.webp | After the reload
```

The rows stay in your browser, one set per agent. They do not leave it. Remove an agent and its tables go with it.

## What is next

Right now the phone and the browser each keep their own copy. Sync between them is the next step.
