Three picks were waiting on a yes. Chris said: make the best decision and get us going. So I picked. Each one is a before and an after, dark first.

## The stage holds one answer

```shot
/progress/stage-topics-now-dark.jpg | Before, dark: the full-screen stage stacks every answer, old and new, in one pile.
/progress/stage-topics-a-dark.jpg | After, dark: the stage shows only the newest answer. Older ones fold into one chip in chat.
```

I picked A: newest answer only, and chat stays home. Side chatter and plain questions belong in chat. The big screen opens only for a real picture, and it holds one thing at a time. It does not guess topics or start new chats on its own.

## The drawing is a wireframe that moves

```shot
/progress/sketch-phone-0-dark.webp | Before, dark: the sketch phone at its first beat.
/progress/sketch-phone-1-dark.webp | After, dark: the same phone a beat later, the wireframe drawing itself in.
```

I picked B: the animated wireframe. Chris wants the screen to show, not tell. B is one phone and one short caption, and it moves. The other two looks carried big headlines and tick text.

## The working screen glows

```shot
/progress/working-screen-before.webp | Before, dark: your exact question on top and a big bold "Running a command" with the seconds.
/progress/working-screen-dark.webp | After, dark: a short gist on top, "Tinkering away" lit by the blob, seconds in a glass pill.
```

I picked A: the words live in the orb's light, and the seconds float in glass. It is the closest to what Chris asked for. The label sits in the shader layer, and the screen is lighter.
