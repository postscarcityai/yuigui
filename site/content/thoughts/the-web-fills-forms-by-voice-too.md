The phone app already lets you [say it and the form fills](/thoughts/say-it-and-the-form-fills). Now the web app does it too. Hold the mic, say a field's name and its answer, and the field fills.

## The filled form

Each answer lands in its field with a small mic mark, so you can see what you spoke. Nothing is sent until you press Next.

```shot
/progress/yui283-web-voice-filled-dark.jpg | Dark mode: three filled fields, each with a mic mark, and the hold-to-talk mic below.
```

## The plan page bar

Plan pages get the same bar. The mic, T and plus sit under Back and Next, so you can talk, type or add something without leaving the page.

```shot
/progress/yui283-web-plan-bar-dark.jpg | Dark mode: a plan page with Back and Next, and the mic, T and plus under them.
```

## Phone and web, side by side

Same mic, same marks, same rule: you check it, then you send it. If your browser has no speech recognition, like Firefox, you get no mic at all. Never a broken one.

```shot

```
