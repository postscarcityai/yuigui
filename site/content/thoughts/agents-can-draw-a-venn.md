Until now an agent could draw boxes and lines. Now it can draw the thing it means. Three circles that overlap. A hill with rings. A flood zone with a note pointing at it.

## Where circles cross

Fill two circles and the colors blend where they meet. Put a word in the middle and it names the overlap. That is a Venn. Drag the line to see dark and light.

```compare
before: /progress/yui291-390-dark-1.webp | Dark
after: /progress/site192-390-light-1.webp | Light
```

## Rings, loops and pointers

Closed shapes, rings that nest from a center, arrows that bend, a bracket for a range, a callout with a line to any spot. It is all plain shape lines, so an agent writes it the way it writes everything else.


A plan page can use a drawing as its picture too. So an agent can show the overlap, then ask the question under it.
