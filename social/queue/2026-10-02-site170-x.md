Opening Yui in a browser for the second time used to take 2 seconds before you saw your chat.

Now it is under half a second. Three changes did it.
---
One: carry less script. Math, music, games and diagrams load only once you use them. 466 KB down to 359 KB.
---
Two: keep each chat's newest 40 messages on your device. A repeat visit draws them first, then swaps in the live ones. 2057 ms down to 452 ms.
---
Three: a first visit used to make four server trips in a row. Now three of them leave together. 4024 ms down to 3013 ms.

What did not move, and why: yuigui.com/thoughts
