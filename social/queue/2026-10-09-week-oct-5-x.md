Ask an agent how something works and you used to get a paragraph. This week Yui's agent answers with a film: a drawing that moves, full screen, on the phone. First frame in about six seconds.
---
Three TestFlight releases this week. A mic on every field you can type in. Decks that fill the screen and morph page to page. Then films that talk. Small models on a laptop can draw screens and films too.
---
Then a bigger goal: not app chrome with content inside, but one living canvas. Every mark the agent draws is live. Tap a bar and it answers. Hold it and only that bar redraws. Drag it and the agent sees where it went.
---
The honest part: the canvas is web only and runs on canned replies. No real agent drives it yet. About ten tries to make films start faster or look better missed, and were reverted. Each one is written up with its numbers.
