If you run a Hermes agent, it can answer you on your iPhone with screens you tap instead of text you read.

Setup is three steps: add an agent in the Yui app and get a code, install the plugin on your computer, pair and restart the gateway. The video walks through it in 60 seconds with the same commands as yuigui.com/start.

Your agent, your model, your memory. Yui is the screen. It is open source and in public beta on TestFlight.
