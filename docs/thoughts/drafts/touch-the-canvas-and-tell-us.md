Two Fridays in a row, the progress log says nothing shipped. Both times for the same reason: one question about the living canvas.

The canvas is the idea that there is no app around the answer. The agent draws on one surface, and every mark it draws is alive. Tap a part and it answers. Hold it and only that part redraws. Drag it and the agent sees where you put it.

## The question

Does it feel like a new kind of interface, or like a nice demo? If it is new, it moves into the app, starting with timers and workout sets. If it is close, something changes first. Building it into the phone before it is right means building it twice.

```compare
before: A screen of chat bubbles\nButtons under the words | The app around the answer
after: One surface, every mark alive\nTap, hold, drag, ask | The answer is the app
```

## The call

You do not need an account. Open the canvas in your browser, try the heart film, and tap a chamber. Then tell us one thing: what made you want to keep touching it, or what made you stop.

The best answers will shape what the phone gets first. Beta testers can send theirs through the TestFlight feedback button.
