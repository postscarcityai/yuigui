Before, the line sat alone and you tapped to reach its question. After, they share one screen.

## What was wrong

An agent says one short thing, then asks a question about it. The stage drew the line on one page and the question on the next. Neither page was full. The tap between them did nothing.

## What it does now

A short line or drawing now sits on the same screen as the question it asks about. The answers are right under it.

```compare
before: /progress/t_a7b-before-light.webp | Before, light: the line alone, 1 of 2
after: /progress/t_a7b-after-light.webp | After, light: line, question and answers together
```

## What still gets its own page

A long page, a map, a game or a timer keeps its own screen. Only short things move up.

The agent guide has the same rule, with a before and after example, so agents write for it from the start. It rides the next daily build.
