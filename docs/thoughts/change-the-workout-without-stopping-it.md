A plan on a page is fixed. Your knee hurts, the rack is taken, you feel strong. The old way was to leave the workout, go back to the chat and ask for something else.

Now you stay in the runner. Tap Edit in the middle of a set and change what you need.

```shot
/progress/yui305-edit-dark.webp | Dark mode: the Edit sheet over the runner, with swap, sets, reps and weight
/progress/yui305-edit-light.webp | Light mode: the same Edit sheet over the runner
```

## What you can change

Swap a move for another. Change the sets, the reps or the weight. Add a move. Skip one. The runner updates at once and the clock keeps going.

## Music, in reach

A small strip sits on the runner: play or pause, back, next. It only shows when something is playing. You do not leave the workout to skip a song.

```shot
/progress/yui305-music-dark.webp | Dark mode: the runner with a music strip, play and pause, back and next
/progress/yui305-music-light.webp | Light mode: the same runner and music strip
```

## Your agent is told

When you finish, the runner sends your agent one result. It now carries the edits too: what you swapped, what you skipped, the weights you really used. So the next workout starts from what you did, not from what was planned.

It works the same on the web.
