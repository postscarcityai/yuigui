It comes from Chris's notes on build 244. The agents worked, but opening one felt like opening an empty chat. You had to know what to ask. This release makes each agent feel like someone who is ready for you.

## Snap and say

One press takes the photo. Keep holding and say what's in it. Let go and both go together, like a photo with a caption you never had to type.

```shot
/progress/yui166-camera-light.webp | The camera fills the screen over a pan of two fried eggs, one big coral button at the bottom
/progress/yui166-answer-light.webp | Basil answers on the full screen: Two eggs, and the butter counts, with a Breakfast card
```

## A meal without a scale

Send Basil that photo. He answers at once, works out the calories and protein behind the scenes, then posts one breakdown that fits a phone. He remembers each food, so the next time is quicker.

```shot
/progress/yui103-breakdown-light.webp | The breakdown: salmon, butter, avocado, lettuce, tomato and sauce with calories and protein, and a total
/progress/yui103-words-light.webp | A meal said in words: Breakfast, two eggs and toast with butter, as a table with a total
```

## Meet the agent

Open an agent for the first time and it says hello on the full screen. The picker at the top says what each one does. About lists three things to ask it, and a tap sends one.

```shot
/progress/yui167-hello-light.webp | Arnold opened for the first time: his hello fills the stage
/progress/yui167-picker-dark.webp | The agent picker at the top, open: Yui, Arnold, Basil, Gouda, Penny and Quill, each with a line on what it does
```

## Every agent has a home

Each agent has its own shortcuts over the bar: Start a workout, Log a meal. What waits on you is right there. Swipe left for its starter screens: Arnold's week, Basil's calories, Gouda's looper.

```shot

```

## The crew works together

Ask Yui something that is Basil's job and Yui passes you over with a note. Basil is already answering. And your tables belong to you, not to one agent: hand Basil's foods to a Claude agent and it reads the same rows.
