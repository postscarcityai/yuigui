Ask for a film about a roast chicken. Scene 1 draws a chicken, not a blob. The phone now carries the same drawing kit the hub uses, so common things look like themselves.

## The film talks

Each line is spoken when the picture reaches it, with the best English voice on your phone. Nothing leaves the phone to make the sound. Pause pauses the voice. Scrub, and it picks up at the next line.

The speaker button next to pause mutes it, and Yui remembers your choice for the next film. The silent switch mutes it too.

## The end of a film


Replay plays it again. Another take asks your agent for a new film of the same ask. Change it quotes the film and opens the keyboard, so you type or say what to change.

## Something with no drawing yet

Ask for a giraffe. The kit has none, so your agent sends the parts to draw one and the phone draws it. Nothing breaks, and it works offline.
