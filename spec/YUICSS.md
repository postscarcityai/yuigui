# yuicss (YUI-342)

How Yui looks, as one stylesheet. It styles every agent screen, and its tokens will drive the app's own chrome too, so a restyle reaches everything at once. The gallery at [yuigui.com/yuicss](/yuicss) draws all of it in three agent looks, light and dark; it is the visual contract the app is checked against. Plan: `docs/specs/yl-screens-yuicss.md` in the repo.

Source: `site/lib/yuicss/`. Built file: `/yuicss/yuicss.css` on the site (`site/public/yuicss/yuicss.css`), generated by `node site/lib/yuicss/build.mjs`; the site build fails when it is stale.

## 1. Four layers

1. **Tokens.** `tokens.json` is the one source: color, type scale, space scale, shape, motion. `build.mjs` compiles it to CSS variables on `.yuicss`; `YuiTokens.swift` comes later (YUI-346). `theme.mjs` turns an agent look (the same recipe as `theme accent=... font=... radius=... motion=...`, compiled by `look.mjs` with the app's contrast guard) into the variables below. The agent's look sets these and nothing else.
2. **Classless defaults.** Every element looks finished with no class: `h1` to `h6`, `p`, `a`, `button`, `input`, `textarea`, `select`, `label`, `ul`, `ol`, `table`, `img`, `details`, `progress`, `dialog` and the rest. Buttons are big tap targets in the accent. The first button is the primary one; a sibling after it steps down to a tint on its own. A container's children stagger in with the look's enter motion; Reduce Motion is respected.
3. **Utilities.** A Tailwind-named subset: `row`, `col`, `grid-3`, `items-*`, `justify-*`, `gap-*`, `p-*`, `m-*`, `w-*`, `h-*`, `text-*`, `bold`, `bg-accent-200`, `text-muted`, `rounded-*`, `shadow-*`, `spin`, `pulse`, `float`, `enter-pop`, `delay-*`. Same names as Tailwind, so a model writes them right first time. Arbitrary values (`h-[62%]`) are inline styles the renderer makes with `utilities.mjs`; colors there are tokens only, never a hex, so contrast holds.
4. **Components.** `card`, `hero`, `chip`, `tabs`, `sheet`, `toast`, `progress`, `ring`, `avatar`, `badge`, `toggle`, `slider`, `stepper`, `segmented`, `field`, `empty`, and every preset as a part: `say`, `list`, `table`, `stat`, `timer`, `chart`, `map`, `shapes`, `sketch`, `math`, `steps`, `calc`, `gallery`, `image`, `compare`, `camera`, `mic`, `page`, `deck`, `timeline`, `game`, `flow`.

## 2. The variables

Set on the `.yuicss` root (the body in the app, a frame on a page). `.dark` switches the palette; so does the site's `data-theme`.

- Color: `--paper`, `--surface`, `--ink`, `--muted`, `--line`, `--accent`, `--accent-ink` (text on the accent), `--accent-text` (the accent as text, 4.5:1 on paper), `--accent-50` to `--accent-900` (50 sits by the paper and 900 far from it, in light and in dark, so `bg-accent-100` is always a soft tint; 50 to 300 carry ink), `--good`, `--warn`, `--bad`, each with `-ink`, `-text` and `-soft`.
- Type: `--font`, `--font-rounded|default|serif|mono`, `--text-xs` to `--text-4xl`, `--leading-*`, `--weight-*`, `--weight-heading` (the look's weight).
- Space: `--space-*`, `--gap` (12px, every container), `--pad` (20px, the screen), `--control` (52px, buttons and inputs), `--tap` (44px).
- Shape: `--radius` (cards and pictures: round 24, soft 18, square 10, yui 22), `--radius-control` (round 999, soft 14, square 8, yui 16), `--radius-sm` to `--radius-full`, `--shadow-soft`, `--shadow-lift`, `--line-w`.
- Motion: `--mo-enter`, `--mo-handoff`, `--mo-stagger`, `--mo-beat`, `--mo-open`, `--mo-breath`, `--mo-ease` (motion.mjs), `--enter-name` (`y-rise|pop|slide|drop|fade`), `--pulse-play` (paused for a still look).

## 3. The guard

Every look, light and dark, passes before it ships (`yuicss.test.mjs` runs all 28 sets): ink and muted at 4.5:1 on paper and surface, accent at 3:1 on paper, accent-ink at 4.5:1 on accent, the accent as text at 4.5:1, ink at 4.5:1 on accent 50 to 300, and good, warn and bad each at 3:1 as a fill with a 4.5:1 ink, 4.5:1 as text, and ink readable on their soft tint. Yui's own coral is the brand and ships as is, like the app.

## 4. Rules for the layers above

- If an example screen needs more than two classes on a line, yuicss is missing a default. Fix yuicss, not the example.
- New class names come with their frame in the gallery. The gallery may use only classes the sheet defines (the test checks).
- Colors are tokens. No hex anywhere above layer 1.
