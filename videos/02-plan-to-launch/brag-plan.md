# Brag plan v2: Yui, one project from plan to launch (60s)

## What changed from v1

- The first example is planning and feedback, not a timer.
- 60 seconds, so it can show the most useful features on one real project.
- Dub music with a little wobble and a heavy sub, instead of the bright pop track.
- Lessons from leo's "Make your product videos look expensive (Apple Framework)":
  - **Rules, kept on purpose.** Three colors only: cream `#FFF9F0`, ink `#3A3340`, coral (`#FF7E8A`, `#C23B4F` for text). One font, SF Pro Rounded. The pottery photos bring the only other color.
  - **One shot, one idea.** The phone sits in the center of the frame. Two short lines flank it, one on each side. Nothing else competes.
  - **Room to breathe, slow zooms.** The camera pushes in slowly and moves in close for each tap.
  - **Transitions, not cuts.** The wordmark flies into the app's header. The phone shrinks into the wall of screens. The chat keeps scrolling from one feature to the next.
  - **Adaptive rhythm.** The plan is slow and clear. The montage speeds up. The outro slows down.
  - **BPM sets the mood.** 140 BPM in half-time feels like 70, the "cinematic" range. The wobble adds energy without speeding it up.
  - **Sound design earns its place.** Only taps and the Send get effects. The music does the transitions.

## The story

Kiln & Co., a small pottery studio, wants a website. Everything shown is a real Yui preset, with the same lines the site's playground uses (`lib/yl/samples.mjs`).
