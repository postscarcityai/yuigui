# 13-brand: the brand lab films (round 1)

## Angle

Yui is looking for its mark in public. Six pieces of cut paper become one clean mark, tried in ink, stone, glaze, cloth and a pinch of pop. The films are the brand lab page (yuigui.com/brand) in motion, and they ask the viewer to pick.

## Facts, with sources

- The sketch is Chris's own cut paper (`brand/sketch.jpg`). The six pieces are traced from it (`brand/lab/tools/trace.py`).
- The clean mark keeps one stroke width and the sketch's gaps (`site/lib/brand/mark.mjs`, `SPEC`).
- Today's coral on cream is 2.3:1 contrast; every round 1 palette clears 3:1 (`site/lib/brand/palettes.mjs`).
- Round 1 opened Sep 27, 2026 (`brand/lab/JOURNAL.md`).
