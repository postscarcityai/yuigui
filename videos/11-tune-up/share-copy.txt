Yui 0.4.1 is for musicians. Ask your agent for a tuner and it listens on the mic, ask for a metronome and it clicks in the chat, press Record on a beat and the take goes back to your agent as the sound and the notes. Plug in a MIDI keyboard and it plays the keys. On TestFlight now: yuigui.com
